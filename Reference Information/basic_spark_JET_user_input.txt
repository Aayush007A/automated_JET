	S.No	Name	Guidance	 
	Exception 1	Entries Made to Unusual Accounts	This test identifies entries made to unusual accounts. The identification of unusual accounts is based on the presence of certain keywords or phrases in the account description and/or the characteristic of, or activity in, certain account numbers. You can perform the search using either the account description or account number, or using both.	 
	Exception 2	Entries Made to Seldom-Used Accounts	This test identifies all entries made to accounts to which less than Y lines were posted during the period.	 
	Exception 3	Large Debits to Revenue During the Period	This test helps identify reversals in the current period of revenue that may have been improperly recognized in the current or prior year. It does so by identifying all entries in which the debits and credits to the revenue account subtype result in a net debit to revenue of more than $X. The search is performed on all accounts mapped to the “Revenue” account.	 
	Exception 4	Users with Few Postings 	This test identifies entries posted by users who typically do not make journal entries. It does so by identifying users who posted less than X entries during the period.	 
	Exception 5	Users of Interest	This test identifies all entries posted by specific users (“users of interest”).	 
	Exception 6	Closing Entries	"This test identifies all entries with limited text / weak narration / blank narration recorded into the general ledger during the closing period.  
 
For purposes of this test, the “closing period” is the period that begins the day when the entity begins to prepare and post closing entries to the general ledger (closing period start date) and ends on the date on which the last entry for a period is posted to the general ledger (closing period end date/closing date). "	 
	Exception 7	Entries Posted on Dates of Interest	This test identifies entries posted on Saturdays, Sundays, or holidays. It also can identify entries posted on other dates selected by the engagement team (e.g., dates on which no entries were expected to be posted or dates falling on or around certain events).	 
	Exception 8	Entries with Round Amounts or Recurring Ending Digits	"This test identifies journal entry line amounts that: 
1. Are divisible by Rs.1,000, Rs.10,000, Rs.100,000, or Rs.1,000,000 (i.e., round amounts) or 
2. Contain repetitive ending digits (i.e., recurring ending digits) "	 
	Exception 9	Duplicate Entries	This test identifies entries that (1) contain the same combination of line accounts/amounts, (2) occur X or more times in the population, and (3) total more than Rs.Y.	 
	Exception 10	Entries Containing Keywords in Journal Entry and/or Journal Line Description	This test identifies entries with keywords of interest in the journal entry and/or journal line description	 
	Exception 11	Entries Posted After Closing Period	"This test identifies all entries input to the general ledger after the closing period specified used for Test 6 (specified in the Input form).  
 
This test supplements Test 6 in that it identifies any entry recorded after the date estimated to be the last day of the closing period used for Test 6."	 
	Exception 12	Unrelated Accounts	This test helps identify entries that record a transaction to unrelated accounts. For purposes of this test, unrelated accounts are any two accounts that are not typically used to record the accounting effect of a particular transaction. For example, to record a sale an entity credits sales and debits accounts receivable, not accounts payable. Therefore, a credit to sales and a debit to accounts payable would be considered an unrelated account combination for purposes of this test.
